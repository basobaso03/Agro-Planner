✓ One-Tap Dispatch
3 Automatic 24-Hr TTL & Refresh

The code expires exactly 24 hours after generation. If expired, or if you want to revoke access immediately, simply tap "Refresh Invite Code" to issue a fresh code.

✓ Cost-Optimized & Secure
🛡️ Why 24-Hour Expiration Protects Your Capital:

1. Zero Persistent Backdoors: If a phone is lost or an invitation message is forwarded to an untrusted party, an expired code cannot grant access.
2. Zero Approval Friction: As long as the code is active within its 24-hour window, the invited partner joins immediately without waiting for an admin to approve a queue.
3. Instant Revocation: Tapping "Refresh Invite Code" instantly invalidates the prior token across all devices worldwide.

TREASURY PROTECTION ENGINE

Runway Capital Planning & Strict Liquidity Spend Gating

Zero Overdraft

Most businesses fail because they commit to capital expenses before pledged partner money actually arrives in the account. Capital Seed eliminates this with a dual-check spend gate.

1 Map Milestones & Costs

Use the 5-step Runway Wizard to map out operational phases (Phase 1 Setup, Phase 2 Inventory/Feed, Phase 3 Go-To-Market) with mandatory 10% contingency buffers.

2 Calculate Pledge Splits

The wizard calculates each partner's required financial pledge down to the cent, subtracting any opening cash already adopted from the treasury.

3 Enforce Spend Gates

When logging an expense, the app verifies: (1) Does the cost line have remaining budget? AND (2) Is there sufficient physical cash in the treasury? If not, the transaction is rejected!

● The Golden Rule: Never Spend Uncollected Pledges

Capital Seed ensures that even if a purchase was budgeted, your team cannot overdraw physical bank or cash vaults. You always know exactly how many months of true runway remain.

01. Acceptance of Terms & Platform Scope

By registering an account, entering an organization code, or installing the Capital Seed Android application, you agree to these Governance Terms. Capital Seed is an enterprise resource and collective capital planning platform engineered and maintained by BASOBASO SOFTWARES.

02. Tamper-Proof Immutability Law

Capital Seed is built on strict transparency. Once an equity contribution, runway expense, debt liability, or livestock transaction is posted to the ledger, it cannot be silently modified or deleted. Any corrections must be recorded as explicit contra adjustment entries accompanied by a mandatory audit explanation note. All actions are cryptographically sealed in sequential SHA-256 hash chains.

03. Code-Based Onboarding & Multi-Tenant Isolation

Organization access is secured via 24-hour expiring secret invite codes managed by the organization administrator. Multi-tenant security rules strictly guarantee that no organization can view or access another organization's capital, member equity, or operational data under any circumstance.

04. True Liquidity Spend-Gating Protection

To eliminate insolvency and overdraft risk, capital disbursements require both line-item budget approval and verified physical liquidity on the ground. The engine strictly rejects any expenditure that exceeds verified net treasury funds.
