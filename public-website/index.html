02. Immutable Append-Only Financial & Livestock Ledger Policy

Agro Planner enforces a strict append-only data integrity protocol. Once a capital contribution, operational expense, fixed asset valuation, feed transaction, or livestock lifecycle event is committed, it is cryptographically signed with a SHA-256 hash and cannot be silently overwritten or deleted by standard users. Corrections to erroneous entries must be executed exclusively via audited Contra-Entry Reversals.

03. Zero-Trust Account Clearance & Role Governance

Self-registered accounts default to the Pending Security Clearance Lobby with zero access to sensitive treasury or site records until a verified Syndicate Admin explicitly approves the account and assigns an operational role (Syndicate Admin, Treasurer, Remote Investor, or Ground Operator).

04. Multi-Signature Expense Controls & Equity Calculations

Operational expenses exceeding the configured syndicate threshold ($150.00 USD) require multi-signature approval from an authorized Admin or Treasurer before funds are deducted from the liquid capital pool. Pro-rata equity shares and harvest profit simulations are calculated mathematically from verified ledger contributions.
